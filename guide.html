Field guide: Job Profit Calculator

What each field means, how profit per hour is worked out, and a worked example. Fits one Letter page.

The fields

FieldWhat to enter
PriceWhat the customer pays you for the job, before any sales tax you collect for the state.
MaterialsEvery item: qty × unit cost, or one total. Include waste and extra trips to the supplier.
Crew laborEach paid worker's wage × hours, plus labor burden for employees: payroll taxes, unemployment, workers' comp, benefits, per diem (example rates — check yours with your accountant). 1099 subs: no burden.
Your hoursAll your time: quoting, buying, driving, working, invoicing. Not a cost — your pay comes out of profit.
ShippingDelivery fees, freight, postage for this job.
MileageTotal miles for all trips × cost per mile (fuel + wear; the IRS standard rate is a fair default).
Card feesProcessor % of the price + fixed fee per payment. Stripe 2.9% + 30¢, Square 2.6% + 15¢. Set payments to 2 for deposit + final.
Equipment, permits, subs, otherRentals, dumpsters, permit fees, subcontractor bills, consumables — anything else this job costs.
OverheadYour share of insurance, truck, phone, software, rent. As a % of price, or a flat amount.
Tax set-aside% of profit to park for income tax. Only applied when the job makes a profit.

Tips

  • Blank fields count as zero. Nothing is ever sent anywhere — data stays on your device.
  • Use Find my price to quote: set a margin or profit-per-hour target and get the exact price.
  • Save jobs and compare profit per hour to see which work actually pays.

How the numbers are worked out

Direct costs = materials + crew labor + shipping + mileage + equipment + permits + subs + other
Card fees = price × fee% + fixed fee × payments
Total costs = direct costs + card fees + overhead
Gross profit = price − direct costs
Net profit = (price − total costs) − tax set-aside
Net margin = net profit ÷ price
Markup = (price − total costs) ÷ total costs
Profit per hour = net profit ÷ (crew hours + your hours)
  …or ÷ your hours only, with “My hours only”
Billed rate = price ÷ total hours
Break-even = lowest price where price − total costs ≥ 0
Find my price = lowest price where net margin or profit per hour meets your target
