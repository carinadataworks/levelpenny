Privacy notice

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Draft for review. Have a licensed attorney review this notice before Carina Dataworks charges for any part of this service.

The short version

LevelPenny, a Carina Dataworks product, runs entirely in your web browser. Your business profile, logo, jobs, estimates, invoices and customer details are stored only on your device, in your browser's local storage. We do not receive, upload or sell that data.

What is stored, and where

  • On your device: everything you enter — business profile and logo, saved jobs, estimates, invoices, customer names and contact details, your settings, whether you've completed the welcome tour, and that you accepted the Terms (with the date).
  • Backups you download: a JSON file saved wherever you choose. You control it. Treat it as confidential: it contains your customers' details.
  • On our side: no account, no analytics, no advertising trackers and no cookies are used by the app. The app loads no third-party scripts or fonts. The only thing that is ever sent to us is a founding-list signup you choose to submit (see below).

Hosting

If you use a hosted copy of the app, the hosting provider that serves the files may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the time of the request) under its own privacy policy. Those logs do not include the data you type into the app.

Founding-list signup

The Plans page has an optional form to join the founding list. It asks for your email, the plan you're interested in and an optional note. Nothing is saved on your device. Submitting the form sends only those fields (plus the app name and offer) through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-to-email service, to LevelPenny's inbox, levelpennyapp@gmail.com; FormSubmit's own privacy policy applies to its handling. We use them only to contact you about the founding offer and plans. We don't sell them. Your jobs, documents and customer details are never part of a signup. You can ask to be removed from the list at any time.

No payments are taken in the app. Pricing shown on the Plans page is planned and not billed yet.

Payment links

If you add a “Pay online” link to your business profile, it is printed on your invoices. When your customer opens it, they leave this app and go to the payment provider you chose (for example Stripe, Square, PayPal or Venmo), whose own privacy policy applies.

Your customers' information

You are responsible for the customer information you enter and for having the right to use it. Because it stays on your device, protect that device and any backup files.

Deleting your data

Delete individual jobs and documents in the app, or remove everything by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Once cleared, data cannot be recovered unless you kept a backup.

Children

The Service is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this notice changes, we'll update the “Last updated” date above. If a future version adds online sync or accounts, this notice will be updated before that feature collects any data.

Contact

Questions about this notice, or to be removed from the founding list: email levelpennyapp@gmail.com (LevelPenny, a Carina Dataworks product).
